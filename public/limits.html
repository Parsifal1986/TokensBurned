Devices & connections

A slot is a place for a device. A connection is a successful authorization. They have separate limits.

What happens when a device leaves?

I disconnect a device

The device stops uploading immediately. Its slot cools down for up to 30 days. The same device can reconnect in its reserved slot, but that reconnection still counts toward the connection limits. Disconnecting again restarts the cooldown.

The credential expires

The slot is available immediately, with no extra cooldown. If another device takes it, the expired device must wait for a free slot. Reconnecting the expired device also counts toward the 10-minute and 24-hour connection limits.

I delete my account

Usage, credentials, and the public card are deleted. Existing connection counts and unexpired slot reservations remain. Recreating the same GitHub account connection does not give you a fresh allowance.

A device means a TokensBurned installation identity. Keep its local identity to reconnect as the same device. Deleting local files does not free a server slot.

History & sync

TokensBurned stores aggregate usage. Import range, upload frequency, and display refresh are separate rules.

History import
Up to 90 days

Import an explicitly selected harness and date range. Older stored totals are retained as summaries; this is not a 90-day deletion policy.

Automatic client sync
Triggered by your tool

Supported lifecycle hooks attempt an upload while the client is running. Timing depends on the integration; there is no resident background schedule. Server snapshot limits still apply to manual imports and automatic uploads.

Changes to a daily snapshot
Hourly / daily

Today’s snapshot can change once per UTC hour. Older days can change once per UTC day. Sending more often does not force a new stored revision.

Public card refresh
About 1 hour

Cards and summaries use an hourly cache. A visit can refresh a stale card; publication must be explicitly enabled.

If you reach a limit

Wait until the release or retry time shown by the client. Repeated attempts, switching devices, or deleting the account do not reset the account allowance.

After account deletion

Deleting your data does not reset your free allowance.

We delete usage, device credentials, account profile information, and the public card. To prevent repeated account recreation, we keep only a keyed account identifier, recent connection times, and outstanding slot release times.

Connection times stop counting after 24 hours. Slot reservations end no later than 30 days after deletion, or earlier when the credential expires. Expired records are cleaned up regularly. These records contain no prompts, code, token totals, GitHub username, or raw GitHub ID.
